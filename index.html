Calls we made

Choices that keep the site accurate and legal.

Radio jammers are off

US law (FCC) bans selling them except to federal agencies. That removed Signal Jamming and two WiFi "countermeasure" models, NGTS-W2 and W3.

Under Vehicle Surveillance is off

Removed at your request, because it doesn't fit the business.

Nothing made up

No competitor comparisons and no invented specs. Provisional numbers keep their "*" or "TBC" mark.

Wrong photos left out

Six products whose catalog photo shows another brand or the wrong device show "Photo pending" instead.
